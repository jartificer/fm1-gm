Track 1

Click to add a note, drag it to move it, drag its right end to change its length. Right-click or double-click deletes. Shift-click selects more; Del deletes the selected notes, the arrow keys move them, Ctrl+A selects the track's notes. Notes past the song's end are kept but do not play (grey).

On the FM-1

Connect an FM-1 running FM1-GM with a USB data cable (Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer), or open the emulator in another tab.

Writing to a slot saves the song there and the FM-1 switches to it. Its song before stays one press of ARP away (undo). "Song in memory" is what the FM-1 is playing now, saved or not.

FM1-GM is free software under the GPL-3.0, provided as is with no warranty. .fm1song files are the FM-1's own song bytes; songs with more than 8192 notes or settings out of range cannot go to the FM-1.
